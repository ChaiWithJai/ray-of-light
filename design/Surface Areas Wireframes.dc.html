1h8 · Notes drawer
✕Lesson 51♪
1Bonjour, madame.
1Good morning…
2Je voudrais un kilo…
2I would like…
📎 voudrais
✕
Conditional of vouloir — the polite "would like". Blunter: je veux ("I want") — fine with family, brusque with strangers.
Culture: shopkeepers expect bonjour before any request.
→ note is anchored to the exact word, opens only on demand — just-in-time, never a grammar chapter
